Issue #16, round 5. Lemon v4 ("Zesty") comes first, right next to v2: same body, with only the ends changed to rounded bumps. Then come v1, the avocado and the egg (Yolk, the baseline). The plump v3 is under "dropped". The avocado and tangerine are from round 2. Every character uses one shared rig: pulse › legs › body › face(cheeks, eyes, brows, mouth) › hands › fx. Recommendation

Round 5 (lemon v4): v2's vertical body is kept exactly. Only the two tips changed, from pointed to soft, rounded, slightly bulbous bumps, with the stem and leaf on the top bump. At 18 to 22 px the glyph still reads as a lemon, not a tangerine or an Apple logo. (v3, the plump one, misread the request and was dropped.)

Round 3 (lemon + Zesty): the stem, leaf and nubs make the lemon glyph read as a lemon, and the upright attached stem, legs and face keep it away from the Apple logo. Zesty is usable for a free, open-source app you give to friends: no voice, notes or Mac AI app uses it. But DoorDash owns zesty.com and ships an AI app called Zesty, and ZestyAI and Zesty.io are funded companies. Expect noisy search, and check TSDR before any public launch. Domain: usezesty.app or tryzesty.app (no DNS). Zesty vs Zest: Zesty avoids the direct "Zest AI" (fintech) collision, so it's better than Zest. It still has its own AI-brand crowd (DoorDash Zesty, ZestyAI). The verdict moves from "avoid" (Zest) to "crowded but usable" (Zesty).

Round 2 recommendation (unchanged):

Among the round-2 three, I recommend the avocado, with the app/character named Cado (Avie is the alternative). You make the final pick. Yolk stays the baseline.

  • Animatable: the round pit is a built-in voice element. It swells and gets a level ring while listening, so the mic level drives one part, like the yolk does for the egg. The rotund belly also suits a squash-and-stretch hop on "done".
  • Small sizes: its glyph (an outline pear with a solid round pit) is the most legible of the three at 18 to 22 px. It also can't be mistaken for an apple or for a system icon.
  • Distinctive: no competitor ships a mascot. Avocado characters are common in stickers and food apps, so the name has to carry the distinction.
  • Name: Cado has no voice, notes or AI Mac app, though Cado Security does market a "Cado AI Investigator". getcado.app showed no DNS. Avie overlaps a little with small AI-assistant products, and getavie.app also showed no DNS.

Lemon (Lemi) is the close second. It has the cleanest name of the round: lemi.app is parked and probably for sale, and getlemi.app showed no DNS. Its pointed-oval outline is distinctive at 18 px. The cost is that it's a wide shape, so the face is smaller. Tangerine (Tangy or Satsu) is third. The new glyph (twin leaves, legs, knocked-out face) fixes the Apple-logo problem, but a round fruit is still the most generic silhouette. Both names are OK: gettangy.app and getsatsu.app showed no DNS.

vs Yolk: Yolk is still the strongest single name in the voice category, and the egg is still the most unique silhouette. If you'd rather have the avocado character, Cado or Avie is about as clean as Yolk. Neither is a blocker.

Avoid: Hass, Zest, Pucker, Lemmy, Tangie and Mandy, plus round 1's Sunny, Avo, Momo, Mochi, Hum and Pip.
